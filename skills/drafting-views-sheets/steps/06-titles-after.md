# 06 · Titles after changes (đặt lại title)

**Skill** `drafting-views-sheets` · **Standard** `drafting-views-sheets.md` § titles · **Tools** `viewport_title_audit`, `viewport_titles_place`
**Applies to** every view of the job (crop, grid ends, template), or the sheets of the job (viewport type, titles, drawing list).

## Do

After any crop, grid-end or viewport change on a sheet: re-place its viewport titles (`drafting-tags-titles`, card 01).

## Done when

- `viewport_title_audit` clean on every sheet a step changed.

## Skip only if

No crop, grid end or viewport changed on that sheet.
