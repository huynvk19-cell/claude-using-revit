# 01 · Viewport titles (title view)

**Skill** `drafting-tags-titles` · **Standard** `drafting-annotation.md` titles · **Tools** `viewport_title_audit`, `viewport_titles_place`
**Applies to** every view of the job (room tags), or the sheets of the job (titles).

## Do

1. `viewport_title_audit` with the sheet prefix.
2. `viewport_titles_place`: centred under the drawing, just below the lowest content; `ignoreCrop` when the crop is far from the drawing.
3. Re-run after any grid, dim or crop change on that sheet.

## Done when

- `viewport_title_audit` clean on every sheet of the job.

## Skip only if

The views are not on a sheet yet. Stair-view titles: leave them unless a comment asks.
